Yam Porridge

Description

Yam porridge (often called Asaro) is a rich, comforting West African one-pot dish made by simmering starchy African yams in a flavorful blend of palm oil, tomatoes, peppers, and spices. It is typically mashed to a thick, creamy consistency and packed with proteins like smoked fish, crayfish, and leafy greens.

Ingredients

  • Yam – 1 medium-sized tuber (peeled and cut into cubes)
  • Cooking Paste
  • Palm Oil
  • Seasoning Cube
  • Onion – 1 medium-sized (chopped)
  • Crayfish – 2 tablespoons (blended)
  • Scotch Bonnet (Ata Rodo) – 1 piece (blended or diced, optional for spice)
  • Smoked Fish or Mackerel – (deboned)
  • Spinach or Ugu (Fluted Pumpkin Leaves) – (washed and chopped)
  • Salt – to taste
  • Water – 3 cups

Steps to cooking

  1. Heat palm oil in a large pot over medium heat. Sauté the chopped onions until fragrant.
  2. Add Peppe Terra Cooking Paste and scotch bonnet (if using). Stir and let it cook for 8-10 minutes, allowing the flavours to cook.
  3. Pour in 3 cups of water and add the yam cubes. Stir gently to combine. Cover and allow the yam to cook on medium heat for about 20 minutes or until tender.
  4. Add the smoked fish, Terra Seasoning Cube, blended crayfish, and salt. Stir well and continue cooking for another 5 minutes.
  5. Using a wooden spoon or ladle, gently mash some of the yam cubes in the pot to thicken the porridge. Be careful not to mash everything—leave some chunks for texture.
  6. Stir in the chopped spinach or ugu and cook for an additional 2-3 minutes to preserve the fresh green colour.
  7. Adjust seasoning as needed. Turn off the heat and allow the flavours to settle for a few minutes before serving.
